# AIMB-G2 Pro AI Smart Glasses — Storefront Image Catalog & Placement Guide

Inspired by the direct-to-consumer (DTC) architecture of [luhxe.com](https://luhxe.com/), this asset package transforms raw unboxing and product photography into high-converting, studio-grade e-commerce assets.

All product assets have been isolated from background noise using Apple Silicon Neural Engine foreground segmentation, leveled to architectural precision, color-balanced to eliminate tungsten yellow casts, and rendered onto a luxury neutral studio background (`#F8F9FA`) with realistic grounding contact shadows.
